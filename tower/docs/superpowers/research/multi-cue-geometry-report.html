Five Cues, Four Refusals

Line features, vanishing directions, planar constraints, learned matching and monocular depth, each measured independently on 16,618 real Ray-Ban frames against the pipeline's own thresholds and a stated null. Four are refused. The fifth has a ceiling too small to buy. And the largest improvement available is not a cue at all — the pipeline already computes solvable geometry and throws it away.

01Inventory

Two of the six cues were already in the pipeline

The brief's first instruction was to establish what is already captured, so no work is duplicated. The answer turned out to cover more ground than the module names suggest.

Edge and gradient information is the substrate of the point pipeline. Both Shi-Tomasi and ORB's FAST are gradient operators; the existing edge_detection experiment adds only a scalar Canny density, which carries no structure and no correspondence.

The planar cue is the homography, and it is fitted twice per pair — once in summarise_motion as a rotation detector, once as ORB-SLAM's rH. Monocular depth was already installed and benchmarked as a loaded experiment.

So of six candidate cues, two were already captured and one was already installed. Only lines, vanishing directions and learned matching were genuinely absent — and all three are refused below.

02Lines

Present in quantity, absent where they are needed

Lines exist on this corpus: a median of 95 LSD segments per frame, essentially none missing. That is the weakest possible claim for a cue, and on its own it is how a cue like this gets adopted without evidence.

The test that matters is complementarity — a cue earns its place by working where the incumbent fails. So the real question is whether lines survive in the frames where ORB starves.

Why, in hindsight

What starves ORB on this corpus is motion blur and low light — and both destroy the gradients LSD needs just as thoroughly. Lines are not complementary structural evidence on this imagery. They are the same evidence, more expensively.

03Vanishing
directions

The noise floor is larger than the signal

This was the most promising cue on paper, and the reasoning is worth stating because it is sound and the measurement still refuses it. A Manhattan frame recovered from line directions gives absolute, drift-free rotation relative to the room, from a single frame, with no correspondence at all — exactly what survives when tracking does not, and exactly the independent opinion cross-segment registration lacks.

A control reframed the question before it could mislead

An early run reported a Manhattan frame found in 98.6% of frames. That looks like a strong cue and is not. Line normals in a room are so clustered that many orthogonal triplets explain much of the line length — the fit barely separated from a best-of-random null. "A Manhattan frame was found" means nothing. Only whether it tracks the camera does.

Measured against the pipeline's own rotation, with a real null

Two independent references — the essential matrix where the pipeline itself calls a pair solvable, and R = K⁻¹HK which is exact under pure rotation — fail in opposite regimes, so agreeing with both is much stronger than agreeing with either. The null is identity: predict no rotation at all.

Whose fault — the estimator, or the scene?

The corpus contains captures where the camera sits on a table. There the true relative rotation is zero, so anything the estimator reports is its own jitter. That separates a noisy implementation from an absent cue, and it is decisive.

Two salvage attempts, both refused

  • Longer lines do not help. Restricting to segments ≥ 60 px raises the drift (2.86° → 3.12°); at ≥ 90 px yield collapses to 59.4%. The floor is not endpoint quantisation.
  • Temporal averaging only halves it where independent noise would give √10 ≈ 3.2×. The error is substantially correlated: the estimator locks onto genuinely different structure frame to frame, not dithering around one answer.
Scope of this refusal

Nothing here refutes the idea. It refutes the idea on 360×640 Ray-Ban frames — at a cost of 45 ms for the Manhattan RANSAC plus 13 ms for LSD, more than the entire existing per-frame path. This is a resolution-and-noise-floor result and should not be cited as a claim about indoor geometry in general.

04Planes
& depth

One already captured, one that cannot arbitrate

Planes: the single homography already has them

Sequential-RANSAC multi-homography over 351 keyframe pairs. The dominant plane takes a median 79.5% of matches; the second takes 6.1%. 72.9% of pairs have exactly one supported plane, and 78.3% have one plane explaining over 60% of matches.

This independently corroborates the recorded rH finding — "a room is nothing but planes". A piecewise-planar method would be re-deriving, at higher cost, what summarise_motion already computes on every frame.

Depth: it cannot police scale

The one job worth asking depth to do is act as a third, differently-sourced opinion on registration scale — since reprojection provably cannot. The known failure case fits at 1.62 px while being 3.2× wrong. So a depth prior would have to be tighter than 3.2×, with margin.

What this measures, honestly

There is no ground truth on this host, so this is disagreement, not error, and it cannot say which of the two sources is wrong. For the intended job that does not matter: two sources that disagree this much cannot arbitrate each other.

05Learned
matching

Correspondence is oversupplied, not scarce

A learned matcher buys exactly one thing — more and better correspondences, especially across wide baselines. So the question is not whether it would find more matches. It would. The question is whether correspondence is what is stopping the reconstruction.

Two views sharing a camera centre contain no depth information; more correspondences produce more of the same ambiguity. The same holds across segments: of 171 segment pairs, 56 already clear the pipeline's own correspondence bar — and registration admits three. Correspondence is oversupplied roughly 19× against what is accepted.

When to revisit

The binding constraint is the one already on record: 16 of 19 segments refused for span/depth, because the wearer stood still and scale is unobservable. A matcher cannot manufacture a baseline. Revisit after the sidestep walk — deliberate translation changes the span/depth picture, and only then could correspondence become the binding constraint.

06The finding

The pipeline already computes geometry it throws away

While measuring what blocks the empty segments, something turned up that is not about cues at all. 34.9% of keyframe pairs inside segments that produced no geometry are already pairwise solvable. Twenty-two of the twenty-three empty multi-keyframe segments contain at least one solvable pair. Segment 20 contains twenty-two solvable pairs out of thirty and yields nothing.

The cause is two lines of orchestration, both visible in the backend and both deliberate:

  • the chain is seeded from features[0], features[1] — the first pair, unconditionally. A degenerate first pair abandons the whole segment;
  • the chain stops at the first link that does not solve, discarding everything after it.

Since a keyframe is promoted for track decay far more often than for parallax on real footage, the first pair of a segment is frequently the worst available seed.

Augment dominates the baseline on every one of the eight captures and cannot lose a component the baseline had, by construction. Plain re-seed does not have that property and gives up the largest component — which is precisely the failure mode this project has shipped once already and recorded in keyframes.py, and the reason largest-coherent-component is reported here beside the counts.

The added components are better conditioned and reproject slightly tighter. Density is lower — 60 landmarks per solved pose against 114 — which is expected: shorter chains accumulate fewer re-observations.

The honest cost, which is not latency

The whole three-way re-solve runs in 58.5 s of derive-time work, so cost is not the issue. Fragmentation is: 82 components become 317. More fragments each carrying real geometry replace fewer fragments of which most carried none — the right trade on this project's own standard. But it makes the registration problem larger, and registration is already gated by scale observability rather than candidate supply. These two facts should be weighed together before this is wired into the production path.

07Defects

Two defects found on the way

"Not attempted" is recorded as "measured zero"

PoseEstimate.matches defaults to 0. When a segment's seeding pair fails, the chain never starts, and every subsequent pose is written with the default. On disk that is indistinguishable from a real measurement of no correspondence — and it is wrong by two orders of magnitude: those pairs actually carry a median of 205 matches.

This is the same absent-vs-zero distinction the geometry contract takes care to preserve on the wire, not preserved in the session journal. It cost this investigation one wrong conclusion before the images were re-measured, and it would mislead anyone tuning from edges.jsonl.

SQPNP asserts instead of refusing

sqpnp.cpp:274: error: (-215:Assertion failed) ++num_null_vectors_ <= 6
                in function 'cv::sqpnp::PoseSolver::computeOmega'

_extend calls solvePnPRansac without catching cv2.error, so a degenerate correspondence set raises out of the backend rather than returning ok=False. It fired once across the eight-capture sweep — but ModuleContainer treats a non- FrameProcessingError exception as a module failure, and mark_failed() is terminal. The blast radius is much larger than the frequency.

What would change these verdicts

Stated so nobody re-runs settled work, and so the genuinely open questions stay open.

A capture with deliberate translation
Every refusal here is shaped by a rotation-dominant corpus in which 54.7% of pairs have no baseline. A sidestep walk is the only thing that could make learned correspondence the binding constraint — and it remains the highest-leverage physical experiment available.
A higher-resolution camera
The vanishing-direction refusal is a noise-floor result at 360×640. It does not transfer to a different sensor and should not be cited as if it did.
Nothing revives lines on this camera
The r = 0.875 correlation is a property of what destroys both cues — blur and darkness. No amount of tuning decouples them.
A loop closure
Still the only independent check on composed transforms, and unaffected by anything measured here.

Every quality number in this report is self-consistency, not accuracy. There is no ground-truth geometry on this host, and a method that is confidently and consistently wrong would look identical.
